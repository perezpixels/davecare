Superintendent Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need to change our current POS or barcode scanner setup? expand_more

No. DavidCare is built as a non-intrusive patient layer. Your daily POS tills, cash handling, and warehouse receiving remain 100% untouched. DavidCare runs alongside your software via web or tablet.

How do patients connect to our specific pharmacy? expand_more

We provide personalized counter QR stands and dispatch bag stickers for your dispensary counter. When a customer scans your code or receives an automated SMS refill prompt, their app instantly connects to your pharmacy branch.

Is patient health data protected under Ghanaian law? expand_more

Yes. DavidCare strictly adheres to the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) registered with the Data Protection Commission (DPC) Ghana. Prescription files are kept in encrypted private object storage and never shared across tenants.

How are Mobile Money payments disbursed to our pharmacy? expand_more

Payments made via MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, or AT Money settle directly into your pharmacy's designated commercial bank account or corporate MoMo merchant till on a T+1 schedule via GhIPSS.
